english_abstract: >
  A funeral couplet Liao wrote for Lieutenant General Dai Anlan, commander
  of the 200th Division, who was mortally wounded during the 1942 First
  Burma Campaign. In parallel classical phrases it praises Dai as a national
  hero who died under fire and calls for continued resistance against Japan.
  Short but a direct personal tribute from a fellow Fifth Army officer who
  also fought in Burma.
---
真英雄羞白发悲些甚么 想将军胆略崇钦幸精神不死

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;弹雨声中肉泥都是成功绩

<br>
<br>

为民族抱丹心伟哉硬干 料倭寇头颅无几愿敌忾同仇

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;旗风影里血路重寻先觉魂
