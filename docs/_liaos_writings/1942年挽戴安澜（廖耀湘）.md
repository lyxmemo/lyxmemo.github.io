english_abstract: >
  A pair of funeral couplets (挽联) that Liao Yaoxiang wrote, signing as commander of the
  Army's 22nd Division on behalf of the whole division, for Lieutenant General Dai Anlan
  戴安澜 (Tai An-lan, 1904–1942), commander of the 200th Division 第二〇〇师, who was mortally
  wounded during the First Burma Campaign of the Chinese Expeditionary Force (中国远征军) in
  1942 — the Toungoo defence and the retreat north. In parallel classical phrases they
  praise Dai as a national hero who died under a rain of bullets, insist his spirit does
  not die, and call for shared hatred of the Japanese invader and a renewed path of blood.
  Short, but a direct personal tribute from a fellow Fifth Army 第五军 officer who fought the
  same campaign. Reprinted from the ninetieth-birth-anniversary memorial volume for Dai
  Anlan, p. 374.
