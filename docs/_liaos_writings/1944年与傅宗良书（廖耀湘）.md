english_abstract: >
  A private wartime letter from Liao Yaoxiang, then commander of the New 22nd Division
  (新编第二十二师) of the Chinese Army in India (中国驻印军, X Force) in northern Burma, to his
  regimental commander Fu Zongliang 傅宗良. After Fu's run of victories in the Hukawng Valley
  (胡康/虎康河谷) and as the division prepared to clear Taro 打洛, General Joseph Stilwell 史迪威 —
  whom Liao accuses of pettiness and of believing slander from an American named Keren 克伦
  — ordered Fu transferred at once. Liao writes that he almost "shattered like jade" in a
  break with Stilwell but restrained himself for the division's sake; he is sending his
  deputy division commander to confer in person, will report to the army commander and
  thence to the Generalissimo how Stilwell repeatedly changed decisions, kept no written
  orders and commanded outside the Chinese chain of command, and has demanded the American
  instigator's recall. He tells Fu to hand over to his deputy regimental commander Luo, to
  fight on to the last minute, and not to lose heart; he weeps for the bleeding regiment.
  A first-person document of Sino-American command friction inside the Chinese Army in
  India during the Hukawng campaign.

<div align="right">
宗良我兄青鉴：
</div>

<br>

迭获捷讯，感奋全军。方期迅奏扫荡打洛膺功，乃史迪威将军来，气度狭小，听信谗言，坚令立即将兄另调。

气恨难名，本拟决裂玉碎，又以全师整个前途攸关，强自抑压，故特派副师长亲自前来，当面商讨一切。

军长明日来列即回重庆。弟必将史历次决心变更行动无笔记、命令指挥不遵系统与我决心处置实施经过、战果详情呈报军长转呈委座。希兄暂回部一转，面谒军长，部队指挥暂交罗副团长负责，务令争气，速清扫残敌完成任务，为兄增光。

美方进谗之克伦，弟亦已要求撤回。兄荣誉职位有弟及军长尽全力作主，不必受刺太烈。在此意外拂逆之时，应心平气和，立定奋斗至最后一分钟之志向，亦不可以馁气也。余由副师长面达，南望浴血战斗之全团官兵，不禁为之潸然泪下矣。

<br>

手此敬上
<br>
顺颂
<br>
<p align="left">勋祺!</p>
