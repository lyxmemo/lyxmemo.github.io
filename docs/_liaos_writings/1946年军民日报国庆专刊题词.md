english_abstract: >
  A four-phrase calligraphic inscription (题词) that Liao Yaoxiang wrote for the National
  Day (Double Tenth, 双十节, 10 October) special issue of the Junmin Daily 《军民日报》 in 1946:
  "Be the vanguard of the people, the voice of the army; army and people sustaining one
  another, of one heart and one virtue" (为民前锋，为军喉舌；军民相辅， 一心一德). A brief postwar political
  motto from Liao while he commanded the New Sixth Army 新六军 in Manchuria (the Northeast,
  东北), and a specimen of his brush calligraphy — the original handwriting is reproduced on
  this page.
