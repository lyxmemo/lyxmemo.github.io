english_abstract: >
  A four-line calligraphic inscription Liao wrote for the National Day
  (10 October) special issue of the Junmin Daily: "Be the vanguard of the
  people, the voice of the army; army and people assisting one another, of
  one heart and one virtue" (为民前锋，为军喉舌；军民相辅，一心一德).
  A brief postwar political motto from Liao as commander of the New Sixth
  Army. The original handwriting is reproduced on this page.
