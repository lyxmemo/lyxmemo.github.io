english_abstract: >
  A 22 April 1947 calligraphic inscription for the first anniversary of the
  Shenyang Xinbao. In classical four-character phrases Liao praises the
  paper as a vanguard of "revolution," using the pen to "uphold righteousness
  and sweep away wickedness," enlighten the people, and keep pace with the
  times (猗欤新报，革命先鞭；墨张正义，笔扫顽奸；启迪后觉，斯民景瞻；日新月进，时代前端).
  Evidence of Liao's public role in Nationalist Shenyang. The original
  handwriting is reproduced on this page.
