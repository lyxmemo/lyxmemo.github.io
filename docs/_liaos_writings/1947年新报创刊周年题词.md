english_abstract: >
  A calligraphic inscription (题词) of 22 April 1947 for the first anniversary of the
  Shenyang (Mukden) newspaper Xinbao 《新报》. In classical four-character phrases Liao
  Yaoxiang praises the paper as a vanguard of the revolution, wielding the pen to "uphold
  righteousness and sweep away the wicked," to enlighten those slower to awaken, and to
  advance daily with the times (猗欤新报，革命先鞭；墨张正义，笔扫顽奸；启迪后觉， 斯民景瞻；日新月进，时代前端). Evidence of
  Liao's public and political role in Nationalist-held Shenyang during the Chinese Civil
  War in Manchuria, and a specimen of his brush calligraphy — the original handwriting is
  reproduced on this page.
