english_abstract: >
  Five classical-style Chinese poems (旧体诗) attributed to Liao Yaoxiang. (1) "Written on
  the Voyage to France" (赴法途中感赋): the young officer on a cold, storm-tossed sea, ten
  thousand li from a Central Plain he watches for a star. (2) "On Seeing a Film of the
  Heishan Blocking Action" (观黑山阻击战影片后感怀): he blames Wei Lihuang 卫立煌 for surrendering early
  "as Wei surrendered to Cao," asks how anyone could escape the pocket at Heishan 黑山,
  regrets that he did not die fighting, and weeps as a captive of Chu (楚囚) of more than
  ten years. (3) "Sorrow of Awaiting Pardon" (望赦之悲): year after year of flowering and
  falling, looking west toward the Liao River 辽河 and the battlefields, with no word from
  the ends of the earth. (4) "Sent to My Senior Schoolmate Nianguan" (寄意赠念观学长), recalling
  their first meeting in Guangzhou 羊城 and the hardness of life's road. (5) "Joy at Seeing
  My Old Friend Jiao Shizhai" (喜见焦实斋老友). Together they document Liao's inner life as a
  cadet bound for France and as a prisoner and pardoned man in the People's Republic.

## 赴法途中感赋

凄风冷雨异乡行，一夜波涛一夜惊。
<br>
寥落此身家国远，中原万里盼星明。

## 观黑山阻击战影片后感怀

卫帅早降曹，疑心梦朝朝。
<br>
黑山狙击战，口袋岂能逃?
<br>
悔未战仁死，无可论天骄。
<br>
楚囚逾十载，相对泪如潮!

## 望赦之悲

岁岁花开又花落，年年翘首我心悲。
<br>
辽河西望伤心地，寒雨连宵只泪垂!
<br>
战阵而今思往事，白云深处不知归。
<br>
天涯问讯无消息，日暮风沙吹柳堤。

## 寄意赠念观学长

回忆羊城初识荆，几多乡谊几多情。
<br>
人生道上难安枕，柳暗花明路万程。

## 喜见焦实斋老友

实翁家宴喜相见，寄语拳拳问故人。
<br>
我道故人天欲健，回头是岸显精神。

<br>
